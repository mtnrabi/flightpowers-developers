# Skyscanner API alternatives for indie developers

The Skyscanner travel API is not sold, it is granted. Their partner pages say so plainly,
and the plain version is worth reading before you spend a week on an application.

Quoted from partners.skyscanner.net on **2026-09-05**: "Our team reviews each application
and will get back to you within two weeks." Their published list of who gets refused
includes "Businesses with low-traffic websites (minimum of 100K monthly active users)".

If you are one person with a side project, that is your answer, and it arrives two weeks
after you ask. Nothing about that is unreasonable from their side. It just means the
shortlist you actually need is a different one.

## The alternatives, and the gate on each

Every figure below was read from the vendor's own live page on **2026-09-05**. If their page
disagrees with this one today, believe their page.

**Duffel.** A real booking platform, so it can issue a ticket, which no data API can. Their
pricing page publishes a `1500:1` search to book ratio with `$0.005 per excess search`
beyond it, and Stays says "Get instant access" and "Available from your first booking with
no negotiation needed". The gate is not an application, it is the search-to-book ratio: a
price tracker that never books is exactly the workload that ratio charges for. The
[full comparison](/compare/duffel) walks through when you should use them anyway.

**Amadeus Self-Service.** Gone rather than gated. Checked on 2026-09-05: neither
`test.api.amadeus.com` nor `api.amadeus.com` resolves, `/self-service` 301s to the portal
root, and the `amadeus4dev` org shows 20 of 20 repositories archived with a README that
opens "[DEPRECATED]". The [alternatives guide](/guides/amadeus-self-service-alternatives)
has the commands.

**Tripgic.** Self-serve tiers exist, but their Essential plan at $500 explicitly excludes
the Flight API; Starter at $1,499 is the cheapest plan that includes it, and their page
notes "The activation charge is non-refundable once API credentials are provisioned".

**Marketplace extraction APIs.** Subscribe, copy the key, call it. No application, no
audience minimum, no sales call. That includes ours, and it includes several competitors we
list by name with their prices in
[the best flight data APIs in 2026](/guides/best-flight-data-apis-2026), one of which is
cheaper per request than we are.

## What you give up by not being a Skyscanner partner

Booking. Every option in the last section except Duffel returns shoppable prices and a deep
link, not a ticket. If your product ends in a purchase you own, you need a booking platform
underneath no matter where the shopping prices come from.

You also give up an affiliate revenue share, which for some side projects was the actual
business model. A data API charges you rather than paying you.

What you get is that you can start this afternoon. Our free plan is 10 requests a month,
which is enough to check a route you know well and a date far enough out that the price band
comes back null. A key is on
<a href={rapidApiPricingUrl('flights', 'guide')} rel="noopener">the RapidAPI listing</a>
and works on the next request.
